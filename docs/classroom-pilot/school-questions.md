# Questions to ask Zoe Live before launch

1. **Class:** which grade and teacher? How many children? Any two with the same first name? Are any of our own
   children in it?
2. **Devices:** iPads, Chromebooks or laptops? A shared cart or one per child? Managed by the school (MDM / Google
   Admin)? Can we add a Home Screen icon? Is any website filtering in place?
3. **Network:** is Wi-Fi reliable in that room? Can IT allow `wordstudy.trebor.me`, `www.gstatic.com`,
   `firestore.googleapis.com`, `identitytoolkit.googleapis.com` and `securetoken.googleapis.com`?
4. **When:** when would children use it in school (centres, early finishers, a 10-minute block)? Is home practice
   expected?
5. **Words:** how does the teacher get the weekly list today (workbook, PDF, typed)? Is there a term calendar with
   dates, holidays and review weeks? How many vocabulary words have printed definitions? Is the verse the same for the
   whole school? May we reproduce the curriculum text in the app?
6. **Consent and IT rules:** is there an ed-tech approval or data-agreement process? Who signs off — principal, board?
   Opt-in or opt-out for parents? Are nicknames acceptable? Is the microphone button acceptable?
7. **Champion:** who is the teacher, how comfortable with technology, who covers when they're out, and will the
   principal look at results?
8. **Success:** what would make the principal say "roll it out" after six weeks — test scores, engagement, teacher time
   saved, parent feedback? Agree the measures before Day 1.
9. **Rewards:** are the Star Shop and avatar rewards welcome in school, or should they be toned down?
10. **Exit:** if the pilot stops, what should happen to the data, and by when?
