# Word Study — practising spelling at school and at home (DRAFT for school review)

**What it is.** Word Study is a free spelling-and-vocabulary practice app used for **[Mrs. ____]'s class** at **Zoe Live**,
with this week's spelling words and (optionally) the weekly Bible verse. Practice is extra; the paper lists remain the
official assignment.

**At school.** Children practise on class tablets or their own device. Class tablets are shared and don't know which child
is using them. A child with their own device scans their personal card, taps "That's me!", and their practice is saved.

**At home — your parent code.** With this handout you receive a **parent code** (a QR code and a short code). Open
**wordstudy.trebor.me**, tap **🏫 School or classroom → I have a card**, and scan or type the parent code (or just scan it with
your camera). You'll see **your child's** progress — read-only: this week's practice, which words are tricky, and recent tests.
You will not see other children. If your child will practise at home on a family device, ask the teacher about their
student card. **Lost a code?** Ask the teacher for a new one; the old one then stops working.

**What the app stores.** Your child's first name (or the nickname the school chooses), grade, a cartoon avatar, practice
scores, stars and streaks, and up to three recent misspellings per word. It does **not** collect email addresses,
birthdates, photos or location, and there are no ads. Data is stored with Google Firebase (US) and on the device.

**Cards.** Each child's card works like a key to just their own practice. Please keep it private (don't post a photo of it).
Parent codes only allow viewing.

**Microphone.** The app has an optional "say the word" button. It sends audio to the device's speech service (Apple or
Google). **[The school will decide whether to turn this off for the pilot.]**

**Questions, opting out, or deleting your child's data:** **[owner name, email, phone]** — we aim to act within **7 days**.
