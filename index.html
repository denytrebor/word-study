Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-08-26

What this is

Word Study is a study tool built for a family's own kids — or a teacher's own class — to practice spelling and vocabulary words. It isn't a commercial product, there's no company behind it, and it isn't intended for the general public to sign up for — a parent or teacher sets it up for their own household or class.

Privacy Policy

What information is collected

Only what a parent types in while setting the app up: a student's first name or nickname and grade level, a 4-digit PIN chosen for the parent dashboard, and the word lists being studied. As the app is used, it records practice statistics (which words were answered correctly, accuracy over time, streaks) and preferences (equipped avatar, theme, in-app star balance). No email address, birthdate, physical address, or payment information is ever collected, because the app never asks for any of those.

Where it's stored

Everything works fully offline, stored only in the browser's local storage on that device. If a household chooses to turn on "Sync across devices," the same information is also stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), so it can be reached from more than one device. Access to that stored data is controlled entirely by a short code your household chooses to share — anyone given that code can see and change that household's data, the same way anyone given a shared document link could. Word lists can additionally be shared across households on purpose, using a separate "catalog code," specifically so households can share the same list with each other.

Children's privacy

This app is meant to be set up and supervised by a parent, guardian, or teacher for their own children or class, not signed up for directly by a child. No advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics services are used, and nothing collected here is ever sold or shared with third parties. The only outside service involved is Google Firebase, used strictly as private storage for the household or class that chooses to enable sync — never for advertising or analytics purposes.

Deleting your data

Clearing your browser's site data for this app removes everything stored locally on that device. If cross-device sync was turned on, a copy also lives in Firebase; there is currently no self-service "delete my household" button in the app itself, so reach out using the contact info below to request that a household's stored data be deleted.

Being honest about the security model

Access here is by shared code, not username-and-password accounts. That's a deliberate tradeoff to keep the app simple for a family to use, not an oversight — but it does mean anyone who has your household's code has full access to what's stored under it, the same as anyone holding a spare house key. Don't share your household code outside your own family or class.

Terms of Use

This app is provided as-is, for personal family use, with no warranty of any kind — including no guarantee that it will always be available, that stored data will never be lost, or that it is free of bugs. It runs in part on a free tier of Google Firebase, which has its own usage limits; if those are ever exceeded the sync feature may stop working until the next day, though the app continues to work offline either way.

Please don't use the shared word-catalog feature to upload content that isn't an actual word list, or anything inappropriate, abusive, or illegal — it's meant to be shared between families using the app for its intended purpose. This app and how it works may change or be discontinued at any time without notice.

Contact

📚 Connect a Word Catalog

Word lists live in a catalog, organized by grade and week — separate from your private scores. Keep it just for your household, or give it a memorable code (like your school's name) to share the same lists with another family. Their scores stay completely separate from yours either way.

If the code doesn't exist yet, it'll be created. Leave it blank to get a private one just for your household.

📚 Manage Word Catalog

One shared catalog can hold every grade in a whole school side by side. Paste a whole year at once — start each grade with a line like GRADE 7 (starts 2026-09-01), then list words for each week — separate weeks with a blank line. Add a comma and a definition for vocab practice. Paste as many GRADE blocks as you need in one go, one per grade. Fixing just one week later? Add a WEEK 4 line right before it so it updates that week instead of week 1 — "✏️ Edit" below fills this box in correctly.
